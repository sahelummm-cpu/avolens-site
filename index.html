Application Purpose

AvoLens is a personal health and nutrition tracking application. Its primary purpose is to help users manage their dietary habits, fitness, and health goals by logging meals, counting calories, tracking macronutrients, and monitoring weight trends. AvoLens optionally integrates with health services to read activity data and provide a comprehensive view of the user's daily energy balance.

Four ways to log a meal

  • PhotoSnap the plate and get a full nutrition breakdown plus a 1–10 health score.
  • BarcodeScan any packaged product and adjust it to the portion you actually ate.
  • Nutrition labelPhotograph a nutrition facts panel and AvoLens transcribes the exact per-serving values.
  • VoiceSay “two eggs and toast” and it becomes a logged meal.

Every AI estimate is editable before you log it. You always have the final say on what goes in your food diary.

A calorie tracker that understands GLP-1

If you take a GLP-1 medication, AvoLens tracks it alongside your nutrition instead of making you run two apps. The feature is completely optional and stays hidden unless you turn it on.

  • Log injections or oral doses using the published dose ladder for your medication
  • Dose reminders that follow your own schedule, with a missed-dose follow-up
  • Injection site rotation suggestions
  • Medication level over time, with injection markers on your weight chart
  • Adherence streak across your last 8 cycles
  • Protein tracking, which matters more on a GLP-1

Supports semaglutide, tirzepatide, liraglutide, dulaglutide and exenatide, including compounded and telehealth prescriptions. AvoLens is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any pharmaceutical manufacturer.

Built to stay out of your data

No ads. No trackers. No analytics SDK.

AvoLens ships with no advertising, analytics or attribution library of any kind. The app runs fully offline with your data on your device; an account is optional and only adds cloud sync and the AI Coach. You can export everything to CSV, or delete your account and all its data in one tap.

Common questions

Does AvoLens work offline?

Yes. AvoLens runs fully offline with your data stored on your device. An account is optional and only adds cloud sync and the AI Coach. AI photo, label and voice logging need a connection, because the analysis runs on a server.

Are my meal photos stored anywhere?

No. A meal photo is sent to the AI provider, analysed, and the result comes back. AvoLens does not save that photo to any server or database. Progress photos are different — those are yours to keep, and are stored in a private bucket only you can read while you are signed in. Full detail in the Privacy Policy.

Does AvoLens track me or show ads?

No. There is no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK and no attribution SDK anywhere in the app. Nothing about your usage is sold or shared with third parties for marketing.

Is AvoLens a medical device?

No. AvoLens is a tracking tool. Nutrition estimates, medication levels and coach replies are informational and can be wrong. They are not medical advice and are no substitute for your doctor or pharmacist. Never start, stop or change a dose based on this app.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the app: Settings → Data & privacy → Delete account. That removes your account and its data, and wipes local state. You can also request deletion from the web without opening the app.

What does AvoLens Pro include?

AvoLens Pro gives you unlimited AI scans, barcode, nutrition label and voice logging, the AI Coach, unlimited manual entry, food database search, weight and BMI trends, GLP-1 tracking, water, widgets and Health Connect sync. It is an auto-renewing subscription offered monthly or annually and billed through your app store. Cancel any time in your store subscription settings.
