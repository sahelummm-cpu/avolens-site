AvoLens is built local-first. The app works fully offline with your data on your device, an account is optional, and there is no advertising, analytics or attribution software anywhere in it. The short version Your food log, weight and measurements live on your device. They only leave it if you create an account and sync. Meal photos you scan are not stored. They are sent for AI analysis, the nutrition estimate comes back, and the image is not saved to any database or bucket. No ads, no trackers, no analytics. Nothing is sold or shared for marketing. Health and fitness data is read-only and is never used for advertising. You can delete everything in one tap, or by web request. 1. Health and fitness data With your permission, AvoLens reads a small amount of activity data so it can show your exercise stats and net calories: Android — Health Connect: active calories burned, steps, and exercise sessions. Read-only. iOS — Apple Health (HealthKit): active energy, steps, and workouts. Read-only. How health data is and is not used Data read through Health Connect or Apple Health is used only to display your own activity inside the app and to calculate net calories. It is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never shared with a third party or data broker. It is not used to train any machine-learning model. If you are signed in, derived totals may be included in your own encrypted profile sync so your figures match across your devices. AvoLens never writes data back to Health Connect or Apple Health. You can withdraw this permission at any time — in Health Connect settings, in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health, or by turning the connection off in AvoLens under Settings. Revoking it stops all future reads immediately. The food, weight and medication information you enter yourself is health-related data too. Where the GDPR applies, we process it as a special category of personal data on the basis of your explicit consent, which you give by choosing to create an account and sync. You can withdraw that consent at any time by deleting your account. 4. AI processing of your photos and text Photo scanning, nutrition-label scanning, voice logging and the AI Coach send data to our server, which forwards it to Google (the Gemini API) for analysis and returns the result to your app. The image or text is used only to produce that one response. AvoLens does not store the image or the message. Our scanning endpoint contains no database write and no file upload. The data is not used to train AI models. Google may retain inputs transiently for abuse monitoring under its own API terms. See Google’s privacy policy and the Gemini API additional terms. If you would rather no image ever left your device, use manual entry and the food database search instead — both work without AI. 5. How long we keep it On your device: until you delete the data, clear it in Settings, or uninstall the app. Account and synced data: for as long as your account exists. After deletion: removed from live systems immediately and purged from encrypted backups within 30 days. Billing records: retained by the app stores for as long as tax and accounting law requires, independently of us. 9. Security All network traffic uses HTTPS. Synced data is protected by row-level access rules, so one account cannot read another’s. Progress photos live in a private bucket that is not publicly readable. Data at rest is encrypted by our hosting provider. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password. 10. Two of these are built into the app and need no request: Portability — Settings → Data & privacy → Export CSV gives you your whole log, weights and measurements. Erasure — see below. For anything else, contact sahel.ummm@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days. If you are in the EEA or UK you may also complain to your local data protection authority. California residents: we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by the CCPA/CPRA, and we do not use it for cross-context behavioural advertising. You will never be discriminated against for exercising a privacy right. 11. Deleting your account and data In the app — one tap Settings → Data & privacy → Delete account. This permanently removes your account, your synced profile row, your progress photos, and your AI usage counters, then wipes the local state on your device. From the web You do not need the app installed. See the account deletion request page. 12.
